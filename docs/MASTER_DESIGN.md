## Locked visual principles

1. **Banana-leaf identity** — banana leaves are the signature visual motif.
2. **Palette** — warm cream/off-white surfaces, deep leaf greens, lighter botanical greens, subtle natural accents.
3. **Desktop structure** — top application bar plus left navigation, central feed, and right discovery/context column.
4. **Soft UI** — rounded cards, restrained shadows, comfortable spacing, high legibility.
5. **Bisaya-first language** — examples include Tambayan, Mga Bai, Isturya, Tan-aw tanan and Add Bai.
6. **Community mood** — playful, warm, local and social rather than corporate.
7. **Original product identity** — a familiar social-feed interaction model is acceptable, but exact Facebook assets, branding, distinctive trade dress and pixel-for-pixel copying are not part of the design target.

## Change control

The owner must explicitly approve material changes to the master direction. Normal accessibility fixes, responsive behavior, spacing corrections and implementation-level refinements do not count as redesigns if the recognizable approved identity is preserved.
